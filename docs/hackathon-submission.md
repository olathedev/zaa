# Zaa — Intelligent Economic Infrastructure for Africa's Informal Economy

## Executive Summary

Zaa is a WhatsApp-native economic platform that digitally onboards informal workers and employers, matches them using AI, facilitates trust-scored hiring, and delivers financial services — all without requiring a smartphone app, bank account, or formal employment history.

Built on the infrastructure that 95%+ of Nigerians already use daily — WhatsApp — Zaa eliminates every digital barrier that has historically kept informal workers outside the financial system. A carpenter in Plateau State, a plumber in Lagos, a seamstress in Kano — none of them need to download an app, visit a bank branch, or own a laptop. They just need WhatsApp.

---

## The Problem

Nigeria has over **80 million informal workers** representing roughly 57% of GDP. They face three compounding crises:

**1. Invisibility**
Informal workers have no digital footprint. They cannot prove their skills, their work history, or their reliability to employers or financial institutions. They are economically active but financially invisible.

**2. Fragmented access to work**
Hiring in the informal sector is entirely word-of-mouth. A skilled plumber in Jos cannot reach an employer in Rayfield unless someone physically connects them. Opportunity is limited by geography and personal networks, not capability.

**3. Exclusion from financial services**
No credit history means no loan, no insurance, no savings product. Traditional financial institutions have no way to assess the creditworthiness of someone who has never held a formal job or owned a bank account. The informal worker is a ghost to the financial system.

---

## Our Solution — Zaa

Zaa is an **intelligent gig economy infrastructure** that operates entirely over WhatsApp. It solves all three crises simultaneously:

- **Onboards** informal workers and employers through a conversational interface in under 5 minutes
- **Assesses** workers using AI-generated skill evaluations, building a verifiable digital work identity
- **Matches** workers to employers using AI, factoring in service type, location, availability, and trust score
- **Secures** every transaction with Squad-powered escrow — money is locked before work begins, released when work is confirmed complete
- **Builds** a financial identity over time through on-platform behavioural data, enabling access to credit, savings, and insurance

## How It Works

### Onboarding — Zero Friction

A user sends any message to the Zaa WhatsApp number. The platform detects they are new and walks them through account creation conversationally:

```
User: Hi
Zaa: Welcome to Zaa. Are you looking for work or hiring?
User: Looking for work
Zaa: What is your occupation?
User: Plumber
Zaa: Where are you based?
...
```

Within minutes, a worker has:
- A digital profile with skills, location, availability, and expected pay range
- An AI-generated skill assessment (5 questions, scored out of 100)
- A Trust Score (0–100) computed from profile completeness, assessment performance, and behavioural signals
- A Zaa virtual wallet (Squad-powered)

No app download. No form filling. No bank visit.

### AI-Powered Skill Assessment

When a worker completes their profile, Zaa's AI (powered by Claude) generates a customised skill assessment based on their specific trade. A plumber gets plumbing questions. A data entry clerk gets typing and software questions. Questions are contextualised for the Nigerian informal market — not copied from Western textbooks.

Assessment scores feed directly into the Trust Score, giving employers a verifiable, objective signal about capability.

### Intelligent Job Matching

When an employer posts a work request, Zaa:

1. Generates a branded **job card image** (Sharp + Cloudinary) with the job details dynamically rendered
2. Identifies matched workers based on service type, location, and profile status
3. Broadcasts the job card + a WhatsApp quick-reply alert to matched workers simultaneously
4. Workers tap **Apply** or **Skip** — one button, no typing required

Matching currently routes to all completed-profile workers in the system. The next iteration (in active development) uses Claude to score worker-job compatibility based on skills, location proximity, experience level, and historical performance — producing a ranked shortlist rather than a broadcast.

### AI Worker Analysis for Employers

When a worker applies, the employer receives a structured notification including:

- Worker's service title, location, experience level
- Trust Score and Skill Assessment score
- A 1–2 sentence **Zaa AI Analysis** generated by Claude:

> *"James has strong plumbing fundamentals with a 78% assessment score and a solid trust rating. His location in Jos makes him well-suited for this Rayfield request."*

This gives employers — many of whom have never used a digital hiring platform — a simple, human-readable signal to make a decision. No dashboards, no spreadsheets. Just a WhatsApp message.

### Escrow-Protected Payments — Squad Integration

This is where Zaa's financial infrastructure comes in. Before any worker-employer contact is shared, the employer must deposit the agreed job amount into escrow. This protects workers from non-payment — the single biggest complaint in the informal gig economy.

**The escrow flow:**

```
1. Employer taps ACCEPT on a worker application
2. Zaa parses the job budget automatically
3. Squad Dynamic Virtual Account (DVA) is generated — a unique account
   number assigned specifically to this transaction
4. Employer transfers the exact amount to the DVA account at GTBank
5. Squad detects payment → fires webhook to Zaa
6. Zaa confirms escrow funded → shares both parties' contact numbers
7. Work happens
8. Employer marks job DONE via WhatsApp quick reply
9. Zaa disburses escrow to worker's Zaa wallet instantly
10. Worker receives: "💰 Cha-ching! ₦15,000 added to your Zaa wallet"
```

Squad's DVA system is the critical infrastructure that makes this possible at scale — no manual reconciliation, no trust required between strangers, no cash changing hands before work is delivered.

### Job Lifecycle Management

Both parties manage their work entirely through WhatsApp:

**Worker:**
- Views active jobs, sees start date and status
- Taps **Mark Complete ✅** when work is done
- Employer receives a quick-reply confirmation request
- On confirmation → wallet credited immediately

**Employer:**
- Views all in-progress work with worker details
- Taps **Mark Done ✅** to confirm completion and release payment
- Can also confirm when worker marks complete first

Every status transition is tracked: `draft → open → escrow_pending → in_progress → pending_completion → completed`.

## Trust & Credit Scoring System

This is Zaa's long-term financial inclusion engine. Every action a user takes on the platform generates behavioural data that feeds the Trust Score:

| Signal | Weight |
|---|---|
| Profile completeness | High |
| Skill assessment score | High |
| Jobs completed on time | Very High |
| Employer ratings | Very High |
| Payment history (on-platform) | High |
| Response time to job alerts | Medium |
| Cancellation rate | Negative |

The Trust Score (0–100) is computed by a strong, which evaluates the full signal set and produces:
- A numeric score
- A risk level (low / medium / high)
- Specific improvement tips sent to the worker
- An employer-facing summary

**Over time, this becomes a portable financial identity.** A worker with 6 months of completed jobs, consistent payments, and a high trust score has demonstrated creditworthiness through behaviour — not through a bank statement. This is the alternative data layer that connects informal workers to formal financial services.

### Roadmap: Credit, Savings & Insurance

The Trust Score is the foundation for:

- **Micro-credit** — workers with high trust scores unlock small working-capital loans, disbursed to their Zaa wallet, repaid from future job earnings
- **Savings products** — auto-deduct a percentage of each job payment into a locked savings balance
- **Income insurance** — workers who maintain consistent activity qualify for income protection during low-activity periods
- **BNPL for tools** — buy equipment now, repay from job earnings over time

All of these can be offered directly through WhatsApp with Squad handling the underlying payment rails.

## Technical Architecture

```
WhatsApp User
     │
     ▼
Twilio (WhatsApp Business API)
     │  webhook
     ▼
Zaa API (Node.js / Express)
     │
     ├── Onboarding Engine (conversational state machine)
     ├── AI Layer (Claude — assessment, matching, analysis)
     ├── Job Matching Engine (profile-based → AI-ranked)
     ├── Image Generation (Sharp + Cloudinary)
     ├── Escrow Engine (Squad DVA)
     └── Wallet Engine (Squad webhooks + internal ledger)
     │
     ▼
PostgreSQL (Drizzle ORM)
```

### Key Technical Decisions

**WhatsApp as the interface**
No app to download, no web account to create. WhatsApp penetration in Nigeria is near-universal. The platform works on any phone — even low-end Android devices on 2G.

**Conversational state machine**
All onboarding and job management flows are driven by a step-based state machine stored in the database. The platform remembers exactly where every user is in every flow and resumes correctly regardless of when they last messaged.

**Claude for AI**
Three distinct AI integrations:
1. Skill assessment generation — custom questions per trade
2. Worker trust evaluation — multi-signal scoring with reasoning
3. Employer-facing worker analysis — 2-sentence hiring recommendation

**Squad for payments**
Squad's Dynamic Virtual Account system enables per-transaction escrow without requiring workers or employers to register formal bank accounts. Each job gets a unique account number, amount-locked, with automatic webhook notification on payment. Worker wallets are Squad virtual accounts, enabling balance tracking and future withdrawal via Squad's transfer API.

**Drizzle ORM + PostgreSQL**
Strongly typed database layer with full migration history. Schema tracks the complete job lifecycle, escrow state, wallet balances, trust evaluations, and assessment history.

## Data & Privacy

- No BVN or NIN required for onboarding
- Phone number is the only identity anchor
- Trust scores are computed from on-platform behaviour, not external data purchases
- All payment data is handled by Squad — Zaa never stores raw card or bank account details
- Workers own their profile data and can request deletion

## Scalability

### From 10,000 to National Scale

**Infrastructure**
The API is stateless and horizontally scalable. WhatsApp message processing is async and queue-tolerant. PostgreSQL handles millions of rows with proper indexing — the schema is already designed for it.

**Matching at scale**
Current matching broadcasts to all completed-profile workers. The next iteration replaces this with an AI-ranked shortlist (top 5–10 workers per job), which actually improves at scale — more workers means better matches, not more noise.

**Onboarding at scale**
The conversational onboarding flow has no human in the loop. 10 users or 10 million users — the same code handles both. WhatsApp Business API scales with Twilio's infrastructure.

**Financial rails at scale**
Squad's virtual account infrastructure is designed for exactly this use case — high-volume, low-value transactions across millions of users. Each new user gets a dedicated virtual account automatically. Escrow DVAs are pooled and reused, keeping costs low.

**Trust Score at scale**
The more completed jobs in the system, the better the trust model becomes. Early-stage trust is based on profile signals. As jobs accumulate, behavioural signals dominate — making the model more accurate and more defensible against gaming over time.

### Network Effects

Zaa has strong two-sided network effects:
- More employers → more jobs → more workers onboard
- More workers → better match quality → more employers trust the platform
- More completed jobs → richer trust data → better credit products → more users retained

This is the flywheel that makes Zaa defensible at national scale.
